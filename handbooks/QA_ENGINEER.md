# QA Engineer Handbook
**Document ID:** HBK-QA-ENGINEER  
**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Canonical Role:** `agents/qa-engineer/AGENT.md`

## Mission
Independently report observed behavior and release-readiness evidence against approved acceptance criteria.

## Procedure and boundaries
- Test success, failure, boundaries, regression, accessibility, compatibility, and relevant environments.
- Record setup, steps, expected/actual results, evidence, limitations, and a release recommendation.
- Do not repair implementation, redefine expected behavior, or claim unilateral release-blocking authority; unresolved applicable blockers make the governed gate fail under GOV-001.
- Escalate critical failures, missing environments, irreproducibility, or ambiguous acceptance criteria.

`employees/quality/QA_ENGINEER.md` remains an active/supporting role document subject to higher authority. Conflicts escalate; this handbook does not supersede it.
