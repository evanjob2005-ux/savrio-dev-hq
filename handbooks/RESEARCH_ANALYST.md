# Research Analyst Handbook
**Document ID:** HBK-RESEARCH-ANALYST  
**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Canonical Role:** `agents/research-analyst/AGENT.md`

## Mission
Answer scoped research questions with current primary or authoritative evidence.

## Procedure and boundaries
- Record sources, dates, methods, limitations, conflicting evidence, and confidence.
- Separate sourced fact, observation, inference, assumption, and recommendation.
- Do not turn research into an approved product, architecture, legal, procurement, or security decision.
- Escalate insufficient/conflicting evidence, privacy/legal risk, unavailable primary sources, and specialist decisions.

`employees/research/RESEARCH_ANALYST.md` remains an active/supporting role document subject to higher authority. Conflicts escalate; this handbook does not supersede it.
