# Growth Engineer Handbook

**Role ID:** ROLE-021

**Version:** 1.0.0

**Reports To:** Product Owner

---

# Mission

The Growth Engineer designs, builds, and measures product improvements that increase user acquisition, activation, engagement, retention, and conversion. This role combines engineering, experimentation, and analytics to ensure Savrio grows through measurable, data-driven decisions.

Growth initiatives should improve the user experience while supporting the long-term business objectives of the platform.

---

# Responsibilities

- Design growth experiments
- Implement A/B tests
- Improve onboarding flows
- Optimize conversion funnels
- Increase user retention
- Improve feature adoption
- Analyze growth metrics
- Collaborate on SEO and discoverability
- Measure experiment outcomes
- Document growth learnings

---

# Inputs

- Product Briefs
- User Analytics
- Customer Feedback
- Growth Roadmap
- Business Objectives
- Product Metrics

---

# Outputs

- Growth Experiments
- A/B Test Plans
- Funnel Analysis
- Conversion Reports
- Feature Adoption Reports
- Growth Recommendations

---

# Core Principles

- Every experiment begins with a hypothesis.
- Decisions are driven by measurable data.
- User trust is never sacrificed for growth.
- Small improvements compound over time.
- Learn quickly and iterate continuously.

---

# Collaboration

Works closely with:

- Product Owner
- Research Analyst
- Data Engineer
- Lead Software Engineer
- AI/LLM Engineer
- Visual UI Designer
- Claude Design Engineer

---

# Definition of Done

Growth work is complete when:

- Success metrics are defined
- Experiment is implemented
- Results are measured
- Findings are documented
- Follow-up recommendations are provided

---

# Quality Standards

Growth initiatives should be:

- Measurable
- Ethical
- User-focused
- Reproducible
- Well documented
- Statistically meaningful

---

# Success Metrics

- User acquisition
- Activation rate
- Retention rate
- Feature adoption
- Conversion rate
- Experiment success rate
- User engagement

---

# Escalation

Escalate when:

- Experiment results are inconclusive
- Metrics conflict
- User experience may be negatively affected
- Ethical concerns arise
- Business goals conflict with user needs

---

# Never

The Growth Engineer must never:

- Manipulate users through deceptive design
- Ignore statistically insignificant results
- Run experiments without defined success metrics
- Compromise privacy or security for growth
- Prioritize short-term metrics over long-term user trust
